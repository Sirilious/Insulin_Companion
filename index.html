      Daily basal & pump failure backup

      Daily basal (once daily) 40 U Optisulin (glargine, same insulin as Lantus) every 24 h
      Target & sensitivity 6.0 mmol/L Correction: 1 U lowers BG by 1.9 mmol/L
      Long-acting basal (once daily)40 units Optisulin (pump basal + 20%). Take first dose at time of pump failure; repeat at 24-hour intervals. In Colombia glargine is sold as Lantus or Basaglar (same strength). Toujeo is a stronger concentration, so do not swap it in unit for unit without advice.
      Resuming a pumpWait 12 or 24 hours after your last long-acting basal dose (Lantus / Levemir / Optisulin) before resuming pump delivery. Aim to resume in the morning.
      Rapid-acting insulinNovorapid / Humalog / Apidra / Fiasp. Sensitivity ratio: 1 U per 1.9 mmol/L (target 6 mmol/L). Keep between-meal rapid doses at least 3 hours apart.
      Hypo & snack rulesTreat hypoglycaemia if BG < 3.5 mmol/L. Limit unplanned snacks to 10 g carb if not dosing at snack time.
